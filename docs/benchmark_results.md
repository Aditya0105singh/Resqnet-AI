# Benchmark results

Generated by `python scripts/run_benchmarks.py 10`. Seeds 1–10 (10 per cell), 180 ticks per run.
All strategies share each seed's incidents, reports, failure schedule, cost function, shelter agents and safety shield.
Cells show **mean [95% bootstrap CI]**. Raw per-seed rows are in `data/evaluation/benchmarks/`.

A paired difference is marked **significant** when the 95% CI of the per-seed difference excludes zero. With 10 seeds that is a modest bar: treat it as evidence, not proof. CIs are omitted below 5 seeds.
